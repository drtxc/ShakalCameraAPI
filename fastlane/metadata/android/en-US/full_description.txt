ShakalCamera — a camera that instantly produces "shakal" quality photos and videos.

11 levels of degradation — from "almost decent" (1280×720) to "4 shades of gray with Bayer dithering" (128×112).

Making your photos beautiful.

Features:
• Live preview with effects (OpenGL ES 3.0)
• Photo and video with degraded quality
• Front/back camera switch
• Level slider 1–10 and 12
• Save to gallery
• Remember settings

No ads. No analytics. No internet. Free software (GPL-3.0).
